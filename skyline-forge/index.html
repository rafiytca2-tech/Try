How to build

  • TapDrop the floor. It falls straight down from where it hangs.
  • HoldThe crane swings faster and your multiplier climbs from ×1.0 to ×4.0. Let go to drop.
  • Hold + swipe ↓Power Drop. Lands harder for ×1.25 to ×2.5 more. A faster swipe hits harder.
  • Hold + swipe ↑Recall the floor for another swing. Costs one step of your combo.
  • 3 secondsForced release. Hold too long and the crane lets go on its own.
  • KeyboardHold Space, release to drop. ↓ for Power Drop (Shift ↓ for maximum), ↑ to recall.

Land floors dead centre for a Perfect. Sloppy floors make the whole building sway, and a floor whose centre lands past the edge tips off. Three misses and the round is over.
