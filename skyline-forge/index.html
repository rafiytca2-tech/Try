Skyline Forge

Build the Skyline Residence floor by floor. Hold to swing faster and raise the stakes, slam floors home with a Power Drop, and keep 50 floors standing.

How to build

  • TapNormal drop. The floor falls straight down.
  • HoldThe crane swings faster and your multiplier climbs from ×1.0 to ×4.0. Let go to drop.
  • Hold + swipe ↓Power Drop. Lands harder for ×1.25 to ×2.5 more. A faster swipe hits harder.
  • Hold + swipe ↑Recall the floor for another swing. Costs one step of your Perfect chain.
  • 3 secondsForced release. Hold too long and the crane lets go on its own.
  • KeyboardHold Space to charge, release to drop. ↓ for Power Drop (Shift ↓ for maximum), ↑ to recall.

Sloppy floors make the whole building sway. If the top starts to lean, counterbalance by placing the next floors toward the other side.
